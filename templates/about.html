Digital Platform for Centralized Alumni Data Management and Engagement

Problem Statement & Vision

College alumni records are often fragmented across spreadsheets, social networks, and legacy databases. This causes major challenges in identifying suitable mentors for current students, manual event organization, poor communication channels, and difficulty tracking alumni career achievements.

AlumniConnect addresses these challenges by offering a single, centralized web ecosystem featuring intelligent AI-powered mentorship matching, job/internship recommendations, automated event management, and real-time engagement analytics.

For Students

Discover mentors, get matched via AI vector similarity, apply for internships, and join college events.

For Alumni

Share professional expertise, guide juniors, post job/internship openings, and showcase achievements.

For Administration

Monitor engagement through 10 Plotly charts, manage alumni verifications, publish announcements, and track events.
